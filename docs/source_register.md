# Data Source Register

## Source 1: P&G Financial Highlights

- Publisher: The Procter & Gamble Company
- Source: Official FY2026 Annual Report – Financial Highlights
- URL: https://us.pg.com/annualreport2026/financial-highlights/
- Reporting periods: FY2022–FY2026
- Currency: US dollars
- Units: USD billions
- Metrics used: Net sales and operating income
- Verification status: All 10 rounded values in the project CSV match the published financial highlights.
- Important note: The financial highlights are marked unaudited and figures are rounded.

## Source 2: P&G Annual Reports and Financial Results

- Publisher: P&G Investor Relations
- URL: https://www.pginvestor.com/financials/annual-reports/default.aspx
- Use: Supporting financial statements and detailed reporting.
- FY2026 results: https://us.pg.com/newsroom/news-releases/PG-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Fiscal-Year-2026-Results/
- Note: Exact financial statement values may differ slightly from rounded headline figures.

## Source 3: Synthetic FMCG Retail Dataset

- Creator: Beata Faron
- Platform: Kaggle
- URL: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/beatafaron/fmcg-daily-sales-data-to-2022-2024
- Period: 21 January 2022 – 31 December 2024
- Records: 190,757
- Fields: 14 original columns
- Description: Synthetic retail dataset.
- Licence: Must be checked before redistribution.
- Important: Not actual P&G sales data.

## Data Governance Rules

1. Keep P&G financial information separate from synthetic FMCG data.
2. Preserve original source data.
3. Document transformations and assumptions.
4. Identify rounded financial figures.
5. Do not claim causation from descriptive promotion comparisons.
6. Check licensing before redistributing third-party datasets.
